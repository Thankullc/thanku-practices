Tend: Practice Tracker
Tending to What Matters · Thanku, LLC.

I created this app because I wanted a digital way to track my daily and weekly self-development practices; without having to give my data to anyone else or having to deal with AI interaction. I thought others out there may also want that option.

The practices we commit to each day impact who we become over time. Having a simple tool to track your practices (and goals and thoughts) can make all the difference in establishing healthy habits. I have used this type of practice tracking system for myself and my coaching clients for over 20 years and speak from experience — it works.

Instructions
The Five Realms
These are practices I've done or currently do, and how I choose to categorize them. They're meant to spark ideas, not limit your creativity. And most practices touch more than one realm anyway, so no need to overthink the label or category. You're practicing, that's what matters.
🏃Body
strength training, HIIT, yoga, hiking, walking, dance, stretching, martial arts, cold exposure, breathwork
📚Mind
reading, learning, creative writing, chess, a new language, a podcast that challenges you, poetry, a rich conversation
🌳Stillness
meditation, breathwork, time in nature, HRV training, prayer, devotional practice, tending to another, setting tech aside for dedicated periods
👤Shadow
journaling, therapy, self-reflection, honest conversation, sitting with what's uncomfortable
💡Extra
anything that doesn't fit neatly elsewhere — time spent in community, with family, or on new projects that engage your creativity and intuition
Your Data
💾 Export
Because I don't want your data, you have to tend to it. To save your data, tap Export — this downloads a backup file to your device. Store it somewhere safe (email it to yourself, save to cloud storage) and export regularly so you never lose your history.
Your exported data is a JSON file that can also be opened in spreadsheet tools like Google Sheets or Excel. From there you can create charts, visualize patterns over time, or analyze which realms you practice most. It's your data — use it however serves you.
📂 Import
To restore from a backup or move your data to a new device, tap Import and select your backup file. This will replace your current data with the backup, so make sure to export first if you have recent entries you want to keep.
Using on iPhone or iPad
Tend: Practice Tracker works on iPhone and iPad without needing the App Store. Here's how to install it:
1. Open Safari on your iPhone or iPad
2. Go to thankullc.github.io/thanku-practices
3. Tap the Share button (the box with an arrow pointing up)
4. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
5. Tap Add in the top right
The app will appear on your home screen just like a regular app. Your data stays on your device.
Note: Must use Safari — this does not work with Chrome or Firefox on iPhone.
Quick Tips
📋 Practices — Tap + to log a practice for today. Tap the circle to mark it done. Tap ✏️ to edit or ⏱ to log time.
📊 Week & Month — See your practice patterns across the week and month at a glance.
📓 Journal — Daily reflection, mood, energy, and tags. A place to capture what's true for you today.
✅ Tasks — Your two-week rolling to-do list with priority levels.
🗂 Projects — Bigger goals broken into action items with optional due dates.
⚙️ Settings — Rename and recolor your realms to match your language and life.
